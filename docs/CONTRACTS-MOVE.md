## Where it stands (2026-10-21)

- `/internal/contracts` is the staff page. Its list is JSON in
  `progress_trackers` ('contracts-state'), with three seed rows (SWC-2026-001
  to 003). It also merges the Mac's `contracts_state.json` when it runs on the
  office Mac.
- `bot/contract.py` ("draft contract ...") writes the agreement text from
  Company policies (`/api/policies`), POSTs it to `/api/contracts`
  (`action: draft`), and texts people the `/internal/contracts?id=` link.
- `/sign/<token>` is the client's signing page. Signatures are saved to the
  Supabase row, so unlike the old `/p/` proposals they do persist.
- Award (Sales) opens an **agreement watch** for jobs over
  `policy.contract.agreementOverCents`: "Nobody starts before it is signed".
  An owner ticks it by hand. It cannot close from a signature, because no
  signature is tied to a job (PR #46 left it out on purpose).
- **Powell, SWC-2026-001: deferred by Lando.** Not touched, moved, exported
  or re-generated by anything below.

## For Lando: terms that contradict Company policies

An agreement drafted **on the site page** (not by the bot) uses
`src/lib/contracts/generator.ts`. That generator hard-codes three terms:

- a non-refundable deposit;
- interest from ten days after the invoice date;
- payment due in seven days.

`contract.py` reads Company policies instead. These are contract terms, so
the site leaves them alone; the plan below retires that generator rather
than editing it. Until then, draft agreements through the bot.

## The move

1. **Site: the agreement lives on the job.** It becomes a document of kind
   `agreement` (a migration widens `documents_kind_check`), made from the
   awarded job and its accepted proposal. Its text comes from Company
   policies, like `contract.py`'s. The client signs it at `/d/<token>`, the
   way a proposal is signed: a `signed` event in `document_events`, with
   the hash of the frozen text. The signed event closes the agreement watch
   (T112: `watchRecords.ts` gains the agreement case, next to the deposit).
2. **KB `contract.py` (needs Lando):** "draft contract" calls the new
   agreement route for the job instead of POSTing to `/api/contracts`. The
   texted link becomes `/app/jobs/<id>`. The places:
   `sync_portal_contract` (line 336) and the four links (lines 425, 429, 452, 476).
3. **Then the old routes go:**
   - `/internal/contracts` redirects to `/app/sales`.
   - `POST /api/contracts` answers 410 ("agreements are made on the job").
   - The Ops card, More and the old-tools strip lose their Contracts link.
4. **Kept:**
   - `/sign/<token>` keeps opening for links already sent, read-only for a
     signed agreement (the `/p/` treatment from PR #43).
   - The 'contracts-state' row stays exactly as it is, Powell included.
